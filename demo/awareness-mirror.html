Awareness Mirror

Everyone can see the path, except the agent walking it

An agent leaves a long trail of actions behind it.

智能体身后留下一长串行动的轨迹。

Logs, developers and users can all see that trail.

日志、开发者、用户都看得见这条轨迹。

The agent may have the history, yet still lack a usable view of its own path. It walks a maze with its eyes covered.

历史可能就在它手里，它却看不清自己走过的路，像蒙着眼睛走迷宫。

The Awareness Mirror lets the agent see its own path, before every decision.

觉知镜子让智能体在每次决定之前看见自己走过的路。
What the present hides · 1

Order and time

Two patients. Right now, both read 38.5 °C.

两个病人，此刻体温都是 38.5 度。

One fever is climbing from 37. The other is breaking from 40.

一个从 37 度往上升，一个从 40 度往下退。

Same present, different past. The right next step depends on the path.

现在一样，过去不同。下一步该怎么做，取决于走过的路。

We built 81 such pairs. With the ordered path, agents told 78 apart. With the present only, 5. Shuffled, 0.

我们做了 81 对这样的情境。看得见有序的路，分对 78 对；只看现在，5 对；顺序打乱，0 对。

On LongMemEval, the Mirror raised accuracy by 14.7 points on average, p < .001.

在 LongMemEval 上，镜子让准确率平均提高 14.7 分，p 小于 0.001。
What the present hides · 2

Change

A user said “three” in one session and “four” in a later one.

用户在早一点的对话里说"三家"，后来的对话里说"四家"。

Sorted by relevance, the agent answered: three in one session and four in another.

按相关度排列时，智能体回答：一次说三家，一次说四家。

With the Mirror, it saw which came later, and answered four.

有了镜子，它看得见哪一句在后，回答四家。

When the environment changed silently, Snapshot agents looped in all 18 runs.

环境悄悄变了以后，快照组 18 局全部陷入循环。

With the Mirror, 10 of 18 stayed out of loops, and all 10 finished the task.

有镜子的一组，18 局里有 10 局没有陷进循环，这 10 局全部完成了任务。

Six of these runs had no exit at all. Where an exit existed, the Mirror finished 10 of 12, Snapshot 2 of 12.

其中 6 局所在的关卡根本没有出口。在有出口的关卡里，镜子做成 12 局里的 10 局，快照 2 局。
What the present hides · 3

The agent’s own path

Same task, same format. The Mirror adds one thing: the path, the last three steps and how they changed.

同样的任务、同样的格式，镜子只多给一样东西：走过的路，也就是最近三步和它们怎么变化。

In the first steps, both groups fall into loops.

开头几步，两组都会掉进循环。

After step 3, Snapshot agents still fell into 3 new loops. No Mirror agent did.

第三步以后，快照组还在新掉坑，又掉了 3 次；镜子组一次也没有。

The Mirror gives no instruction. The change of course is the agent’s own.

镜子不下任何指令，改道是智能体自己做的。

Where an exit existed, Mirror agents looped 2 times against 13, and they finished more tasks in fewer steps.

在有出口的关卡里，镜子组掉坑 2 局，快照组 13 局；镜子组做成的也更多，用的步数更少。
What the present hides · 4

Drift from the goal, across handoffs

A long task passes through five fresh agents. Each gets a short handover.

一个长任务经过五个新的智能体接力，每次交接只给一份很短的交接内容。

The goal switches from A to B, but later reports make A look right again.

目标从 A 换成了 B，可后来的报告又让 A 看起来是对的。

At the fifth handoff, with recent events only, 0 of 20 agents act correctly. With the Mirror, 16 of 20.

到第五次交接，只给最近事件的一组，20 个里 0 个做对；有镜子的一组，16 个做对。

Across all five handoffs, adherence rose by about 0.3 on both models.

把五次交接合起来看，两个模型的守住目标程度都提高了约 0.3。
