Interaction Field Dynamics
Why a field?

The words stay the same. Their force changes with the field.

Two words: “Go ahead.”

两个字："你来吧。"

Same words, three meanings. What changed is the field they land in.

同样的字，三种意思。变的是它们落进去的那个场。

That field is built by everything that came before.

这个场，是之前发生的一切攒出来的。
Definition

What is an Interaction Field?

An Interaction Field is the evolving dynamic structure jointly formed through interaction, shaping the conditions for what can happen next.

Two parties, a person and an AI or two AIs, each turn sending something into the space between them.

两方，人和 AI，或者两个 AI，每一轮都往彼此之间送出点什么。

Between them a structure forms. Both make it, and it holds both of them.

两人之间长出一个结构。两个人一起造出它，它也同时托着两个人。 互动场是交互双方共同形成、持续演化的动力结构，它塑造着接下来互动发生的条件。
The framework

State, Action, Flow, Readout

State: where the interaction is right now.

State：这段互动此刻在哪儿。

Action: what a turn does to that State, moving it to the next one.

Action：这一轮对它起了什么作用，把它推到下一个状态。

Flow: the path those State–Action–State steps trace together.

Flow：一步步"状态→作用→状态"连起来走出的路。

Readout: what really happened next, recorded on a separate channel.

Readout：后来真正发生了什么，用一条单独的证据通道记下。

IFD makes dynamic interaction measurable by decomposing it into observable, assessable, and traceable variables.

IFD 把持续的互动分解成可观测、可评定、可追踪的变量，让动态互动变得可以测量。
State

What a State holds

Four things both sides share: ground, jointness, and what is left over, good and bad.

两边共享的四样：共同地基、共同推进，还有留下来的东西，好的和伤的。

Three things each side holds on its own: how well it reads the other, how open it is, how alive the line feels.

每一方各自有的三样：读懂对方多少、对对方多敞开、这条线在自己这边还接不接得下去。

And who is leading, following, or stuck. Together: sixteen numbers.

再加上谁在带、谁在跟、还是卡住了。合起来，16 个数。
Action and Flow

How the field moves

An Action has four parts: what it pushes on, which way, how strongly, and how well it fits the moment.

一个 Action 有四部分：推什么（Target）、往哪边推（Direction）、推得多大劲（Magnitude）、合不合此刻的场（Adaptation）。

Flow reads the shape of the path: coupled, drifting, swinging, stuck, recovering, breaking, resonating, waking up again.

Flow 读的是这条路的形状：咬合、漂开、摇摆、停住、恢复、破裂、共振、重新激活。

The same State can be the end of a decline or the middle of a recovery. Flow tells them apart.

同一个状态，可能是下坡的终点，也可能是恢复的中途。Flow 把它们分开。
Interaction Field Dynamics

Interaction happens in a field.

The field carries the dynamics of interaction.

Every interaction reshapes the field and what can happen next.

IFD makes the present that history has created measurable.

交互发生在场中。场承载着交互的动力。每一次交互都会重塑场，也重塑接下来可能发生什么。 IFD 让历史塑造出来的"此刻"变得可以测量。
