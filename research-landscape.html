Celestelin looks like a human–AI interaction project: memory, presence, long-term collaboration. Everything a person meets passes through language, and beneath it sits a set of mechanisms that let a long-running agent stay itself and stay trustworthy over time. Each topic here is a problem a running system met first. Pick a field below to see where it runs through the whole structure. Celestelin 看起来是一个人机交互项目：记忆、陪伴、长期协作。人碰到的一切都要经过语言；语言底下，是一整套让长期运行的 agent 保持自己、长期可信的机制。这里的每个议题，都是一个真实运行的系统先撞上的问题。点下面一个领域，看它怎样贯穿整座冰山。

Fields领域

The sea is the interaction field that grows between a person and an AI. Below the water, memory, trajectory, active work, runtime state and values sit side by side and together form the agent's longitudinal state. PersonaCore, the identity anchor, sits beneath every architecture, and the whole structure stands on a system that has been running since April 2025.海水是人和 AI 一起长出来的交互场。水面下，记忆、轨迹、手头的工作、运行时状态和价值并排在一起，共同构成 agent 的长期状态。PersonaCore 是身份锚，挂在每一个架构下面。整座冰山立在一个 2025 年 4 月起持续运行的系统上。
